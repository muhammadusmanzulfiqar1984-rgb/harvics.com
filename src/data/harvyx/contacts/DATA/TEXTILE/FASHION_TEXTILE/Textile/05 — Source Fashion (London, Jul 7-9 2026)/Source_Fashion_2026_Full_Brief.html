Executive Summary

Source Fashion London is Europe's largest sustainable B2B fashion sourcing event, connecting verified garment manufacturers, textile producers, and supply chain service providers with retail buyers, private label brands, and product development teams. Organised by Hyve Group PLC, the July 2026 edition at Olympia London will focus on three macro themes: market volatility, compliance, and sustainable sourcing — as confirmed by the show's 2026 agenda announcement.

Pakistan is directly relevant to this show. Pakistan's GSP+ status with the UK (post-Brexit rolled-over preference) means Pakistan-origin garments enter the UK at 0% duty — a major competitive advantage versus competitors losing GSP benefits. Pakistan is one of the most active exhibiting countries at Source Fashion, with TDAP-facilitated pavilions at previous editions.

HARVICS / JACOBS & CO STRATEGIC RATIONALE: Source Fashion is the single most important sourcing event for Harvics' apparel channel. With Jacobs & Co's London office, this is a home-ground event. Pakistan's UK GSP+ advantage, competitive garment costs (30–40% below India), and established textile belt (Faisalabad, Lahore) make this the primary platform for securing UK retail buyer orders for Pakistan-origin apparel.

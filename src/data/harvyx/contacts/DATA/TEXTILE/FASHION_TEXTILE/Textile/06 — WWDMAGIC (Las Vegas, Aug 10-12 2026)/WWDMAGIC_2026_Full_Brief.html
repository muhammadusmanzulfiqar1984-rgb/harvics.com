Executive Summary

WWDMAGIC (also marketed as MAGIC Las Vegas) is the largest fashion trade show in North America, encompassing four co-located events: MAGIC (women's trend and young contemporary), PROJECT (men's fashion), SOURCING (manufacturing and supply chain), and OFFPRICE (margin-building excess inventory). Combined, the event draws 70,000 industry insiders from 80+ countries.

For Harvics, the most relevant segment is MAGIC SOURCING — the manufacturing and supply chain pavilion where Pakistan-origin garment suppliers, textile producers, and sourcing agents meet US retail buyers, private label brands, and product development teams. The 74% C-suite / VP+ buyer demographic makes this the highest-value decision-maker audience in US fashion.

HARVICS STRATEGIC RATIONALE: MAGIC SOURCING is the US equivalent of Source Fashion London. Pakistan-based garment manufacturers and Harvics as their MSC can position Pakistan-origin apparel to US retailers seeking post-China sourcing alternatives. The OFFPRICE segment is also relevant — excess FMCG and food inventory traded at this segment could open alternative US food distribution channels.

Executive Summary

Pitti Uomo is the world's most prestigious menswear trade show, held biannually in Florence. Edition 110 (June 2026) presents Spring/Summer 2027 men's collections. The event is the global calendar centrepiece for buyers, retailers, and press covering luxury menswear, contemporary fashion, and trend-forward outdoor/streetwear. Over 720 brands — 44% international — participate across five curated sections.

For Harvics' apparel sourcing vertical (Jacobs & Co London office), Pitti Uomo 110 is the primary intelligence and buyer-relationship event for menswear. This is where the most influential multi-brand retailers, department store buyers, and independent boutique owners commit to SS27 buys. Pakistan's high-quality fabric and garment manufacturing sector is an increasingly credible option for European buyers facing India/China cost pressure.

HARVICS STRATEGIC RATIONALE: Pakistan is the world's 4th largest cotton producer and a competitive garment manufacturing hub. Pitti Uomo buyer intelligence enables Jacobs & Co to position Pakistan-origin knitwear, workwear, and premium cotton apparel to boutique and department store buyers who will be at this show. The key is to identify buyers who are actively diversifying sourcing away from China.

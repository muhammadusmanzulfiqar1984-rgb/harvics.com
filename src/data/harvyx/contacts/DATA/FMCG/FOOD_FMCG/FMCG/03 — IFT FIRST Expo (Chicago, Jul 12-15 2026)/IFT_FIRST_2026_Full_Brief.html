Executive Summary

IFT FIRST (Food Improved by Research, Science & Technology) is the world's leading annual event for food science and technology. Held at McCormick Place — the largest convention centre in North America — the 2026 edition introduces a landmark format change: all scientific programming will take place directly in the expo hall for the first time, creating seamless R&D-to-procurement engagement.

For Harvics, IFT FIRST is most relevant to the FMCG private label vertical and the biodiesel/oleochemical supply chain. Key ingredient suppliers, food technology vendors, packaging innovators, and private label technology providers attend in force. The show is also an intelligence-gathering platform for food processing equipment relevant to Phase 2 IQF and rice mill investments.

HARVICS STRATEGIC RATIONALE: IFT FIRST is not a buyer fair — it is a supplier and technology fair. Harvics should attend to: (1) source food technology partnerships for Green Heavens' Phase 2 processing assets; (2) identify ingredient suppliers for FMCG private label; (3) connect with food safety and halal certification bodies; (4) network with ingredient exporters interested in Pakistan-sourced raw materials.

1. SHOW OVERVIEW — VERIFIED FROM OFFICIAL SOURCE (specialtyfood.com)

The Summer Fancy Food Show is the specialty food industry’s premier product discovery event, operated by the Specialty Food Association (SFA) — a membership-based US trade association representing 3,000+ member companies. Now in its 70th year, the 2026 edition returns to New York City’s Jacob Javits Convention Center. It is the largest specialty food trade show in North America and the single most efficient meeting point for manufacturers, retail buyers, distributors, investors, brokers, and press in the FMCG specialty food sector.

The show runs Sunday June 28 through Tuesday June 30, 2026, 10am–5pm daily (early access for buyer badges from 9:30am). Last year the event drew nearly 2,500 brands with over 900 new products on display. More than 7 in 10 attendees are buyers, investors, or press — making it a highly qualified, low “window shopper” environment.

HARVICS RELEVANCE: This show is the single most important US-market entry point for the FMCG Private Label SPV (Vertical 3). Target buyers — specialty retailers, ethnic chain buyers, distributor networks such as UNFI and KeHE — all attend. Category coverage includes jams, sauces, pickles, spices, condiments, honey, and squash — exactly the Green Heavens private label portfolio. The show has a dedicated international pavilion with a separate contact (Mimo Boulefrakh, Manager Global Accounts, 646-878-0151) for international exhibitors.

8. FOOD DONATION PROGRAMME (CSR / ESG NOTE)

Since 1992, SFA has operated an Exhibitor Food Donation programme at all tradeshows. Each year approximately 200,000 pounds of food are donated to those in need. In New York, SFA works with City Harvest (rescues 30M+ pounds/year). At show end, furniture, household items, and construction materials are donated to NYC non-profits via Freeman and the Javits Care Programme. For Harvics/GH ESG positioning: all unsold samples are donated — zero waste, positive PR narrative for IPO roadshow.

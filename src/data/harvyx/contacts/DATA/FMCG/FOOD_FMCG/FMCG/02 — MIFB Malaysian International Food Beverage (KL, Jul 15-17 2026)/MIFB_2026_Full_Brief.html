Executive Summary

MIFB is Southeast Asia's premier food and beverage trade fair, held annually at KLCC. The 2026 edition — themed "Future-Ready F&B: Powering the Next Wave" — returns with a renewed national focus, positioning Malaysia's F&B industry as a connected domestic ecosystem built to scale internationally. For Harvics, MIFB represents the primary ASEAN market entry gateway for halal-certified products, Basmati rice distribution, and FMCG private label placement across the Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore, Brunei, and Philippines retail channels.

MIFB 2025 saw 500+ companies, 12,000+ trade visitors, and debuted the Malaysian Café Expo and Malaysia National Coffee Championship — confirming escalating show momentum ahead of 2026.

HARVICS STRATEGIC RATIONALE: Malaysia is a 100% halal-compliant market and MIFB is the fastest route to halal-certified food buyers across ASEAN. Pakistan-origin Basmati, halal beef, and spice-based FMCG are in active demand. Malaysia is also a re-export hub into GCC through MIDA-certified facilities — Harvics products entering via MIFB can achieve dual GCC + ASEAN market exposure.

# PART A — HARVICS CORE

## FIN-001 — What is Harvics?

**Short Answer**  
Harvics is building an AI-driven global commerce ecosystem designed to connect businesses, buyers, suppliers, products, trade workflows, supply chains, data, and digital infrastructure.

**Detailed Answer**  
Harvics Global Ventures is developing a broader operating layer for global commerce. Unlike traditional marketplaces that primarily list products and connect buyers and sellers, Harvics aims to connect the wider commercial journey—from discovery and sourcing to RFQs, supplier relationships, trade execution, supply-chain visibility, data, AI, and business operations.

**Keywords:** Harvics, company, global commerce, trade, platform  
**CTA:** Explore https://www.harvics.com/en or contact the Harvics team via /en/contact.

# PART M — LEAD QUALIFICATION

FIN should identify the visitor's intent, then run the matching flow.

### Intent 1: Buyer
1. What product are you looking for?  
2. What quantity do you need?  
3. Which country should the product be delivered to?  
Then: company, contact, timeline → RFQ / sourcing@

### Intent 2: Supplier
1. What products do you supply?  
2. Which markets do you currently export to?  
Then: FIN-030 fields → partnerships@ / sell

### Intent 3: Distributor
1. Which country or territory are you interested in representing?  
Then: FIN-031 fields → partnerships@

### Intent 4: Strategic Partner
1. What type of partnership are you proposing?  
Then: company, market, offer → escalate human + partnerships@

### Intent 5: Enterprise Technology
1. Which Harvics platform or business function are you interested in?  
Then: company, use case → technology@ / human

**Also capture when possible:** name · company · country · role · email/phone · urgency · estimated scale

## 2. HARVICS — MASTER COMPANY PROFILE

### 2.1 What is Harvics?

**Canonical:** Harvics is a global commerce and trade infrastructure company building an AI-driven ecosystem designed to connect businesses, buyers, suppliers, products, services, technology, and operational workflows across markets.

Harvics combines global trade, sourcing, supply-chain intelligence, business technology, digital platforms, and AI-driven systems into an integrated operating environment.

Vision: make cross-border commerce simpler, more intelligent, more transparent, and more connected.

**Site facts to keep aligned:** Founded 2019 · Harvics Global Ventures · 10 verticals · multi-country presence · www.harvics.com

### 2.2 Is Harvics a marketplace?

Harvics can support marketplace and commerce functions, but its vision goes beyond a traditional online marketplace.

Traditional marketplaces primarily list products and connect buyers and sellers.

Harvics is designed as a broader **global commerce operating layer**, connecting sourcing, RFQs, supplier relationships, trade operations, supply-chain visibility, business intelligence, compliance, payments, and other workflows.

**Short:** Harvics does not aim to simply list products. It aims to help businesses operate and execute trade.

### 2.3 What does Harvics do?

- Global trade · Import/export · B2B sourcing
- Buyer–supplier connections · RFQ/quotation workflows
- Supply-chain intelligence · Business technology · AI-driven operations
- HR technology · VAT/tax technology · Event/expo technology
- Digital commerce · FMCG products and brands
- Payment and embedded-finance infrastructure · Partnerships

Exact availability varies by market and development stage.

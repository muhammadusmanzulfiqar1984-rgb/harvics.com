## ARTICLE: La Pres — presentation decks (not company login)

**La Pres** is the pin/code-gated **presentation** lobby for client decks. It is **not** HarvyX login and **not** partner CRM login.

- Entry: https://www.harvics.com/en/la-pres  
- Lobby = general Harvics / project decks  
- Lounge = programme-specific decks (e.g. textiles LPP, FMCG MAFI, Vietnam Denim)

**SOP for Fin**
1. Ask if they need a **presentation** (La Pres) or **operator/partner access** (login / HarvyX).
2. If presentation: send them to `/en/la-pres` and tell them to use the **programme code issued by their Harvics contact**. Fin must **not invent or publish access codes**.
3. If they lost a code: escalate to human / office@harvics.com — do not reset codes in chat.

## ARTICLE: Website FAQ (from `/en/faq`)

Public FAQ page: https://www.harvics.com/en/faq  
Help page: https://www.harvics.com/en/help  

**Note for Fin:** Much of this FAQ is **consumer/retail heritage**. For B2B volume, logistics contracts, or trade finance, prefer Contact / RFQ and desk routing above.

### Products
**Q: What products does Harvics offer?**  
A: Premium food products including confectionery, beverages, snacks, pasta, bakery, and frozen foods across multiple categories — plus the broader 10-vertical trade platform (textiles, commodities, industrial, etc.).

**Q: Where can I buy Harvics products?**  
A: Retail availability is marketed across 40+ countries. Use store locator / contact, or for B2B volume use contact directory / RFQ.

**Q: Do you offer bulk ordering?**  
A: Yes for businesses and retailers. Direct them to contact or bulk order / RFQ paths.

### Orders & shipping
**Q: Shipping options?**  
A: Standard, express, and international options are described on the FAQ; free shipping messaging for small retail orders over $50 may appear — **do not apply retail free-shipping rules to container/B2B trade**.

**Q: Delivery time?**  
A: FAQ: standard ~5–7 business days; express ~2–3. For export/trade, lead times depend on category and origin — collect a brief.

**Q: Track order?**  
A: Tracking number in confirmation email when an e-commerce order exists.

### Account & payments
**Q: Create an account?**  
A: Sign In → Create Account in header; partners may use `/en/login`; HarvyX operators use `/app/sign-up`.

**Q: Payment methods?**  
A: FAQ lists major cards, PayPal, and bank transfers for B2B. Trade deals may use LC/TT/escrow — Finance advises case by case.

**Q: Payment security?**  
A: Industry-standard encryption; never ask users to paste full card numbers into Intercom chat.

### Returns & refunds
**Q: Return policy?**  
A: 30-day return for unopened products in original packaging; contact support for RMA.  
**Q: Refunds?**  
A: Contact support with order number; FAQ states processing within 5–7 business days.

**Still need help?** Contact Us → https://www.harvics.com/en/contact · support@harvics.com
